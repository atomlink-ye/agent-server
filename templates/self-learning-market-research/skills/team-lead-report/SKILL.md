# Team lead report

This skill requires the `lead` tool profile.

Use only completed member work items and their tool evidence. The first lead
turn creates exactly two work items: one opportunity analysis and one analog /
risk review. The final report has exactly these six sections, in order:

1. Scope
2. Snapshot
3. Events
4. Opportunities
5. Analog and risk review
6. Learning loop

Every section must include or inherit the disclaimer **synthetic demo only**.
Always identify `fixture://self-learning-market-research/acme-v1`, `ACME`, and
`2026-07-31`. Do not give investment advice or precise backtest claims.
